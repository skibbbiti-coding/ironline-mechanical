03

Kitchen Ventilation.

Chef-driven kitchens run hot, smoky and long. We engineer the exhaust, make-up air and pollution control as one balanced system — so the dining room never smells like the line.

Kitchen ventilation page Discuss a project

Exhaust

Type I grease and Type II heat/vapor hoods, welded grease duct per NFPA 96 and CMC.

Make-up air

Direct-fired and indirect tempered MUA, interlocked and balanced to keep the kitchen negative.

Pollution control

Electrostatic precipitators and odor control for wood-fire and charbroil in mixed-use buildings.

Controls

Demand-control kitchen ventilation with optical and temperature sensing.
