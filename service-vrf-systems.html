01 Why VRF

Simultaneous heating and cooling, minimal ductwork, maximum ceiling height.

In LA’s mild climate, perimeter zones often need cooling while interior zones need heat. Heat-recovery VRF moves that energy between rooms instead of rejecting it, and small refrigerant lines replace the large ducts that eat ceiling height in older buildings.

  • 2-pipe and 3-pipe heat recovery
  • Ducted, cassette and concealed fan coils
  • DOAS with energy recovery for ventilation air
02 Design-assist

Branch boxes, line sets and condensers coordinated before CDs.

We model refrigerant piping lengths and elevation limits, locate branch selector boxes where they can be serviced, and screen condensers acoustically and visually on the roof. Refrigerant concentration limits are checked room by room against ASHRAE 15 and the applicable mechanical code.

03 Install & commissioning

Brazed under nitrogen, pressure-tested, evacuated and verified.

Our crews braze with nitrogen purge, hold standing pressure tests, triple-evacuate and weigh in charge. Commissioning covers addressing, setpoint and setback sequences, BMS integration and a TAB report for the outdoor air system.

04 Service

Leak checks, controls trending and compressor health.

Plan clients get scheduled refrigerant leak checks, filter and coil service on every indoor unit and remote alarm routing from the VRF gateway to our service desk.

FAQ VRF Systems

Questions owners and architects ask.

Is VRF a good fit for an older building in Los Angeles?

Often, yes. Refrigerant lines are far smaller than ductwork, which makes VRF well suited to adaptive reuse projects with exposed structure and limited floor-to-floor height. We confirm structural loads for roof condensers and electrical capacity early in design-assist.

How does VRF handle fresh air?

VRF conditions recirculated room air. Code-required ventilation is delivered separately, usually by a dedicated outdoor air system with energy recovery, sized per ASHRAE 62.1.

How quiet are VRF fan coils?

Concealed ducted fan coils selected at low static pressure routinely achieve NC-25 or better in guest rooms and private offices. We verify sound levels at commissioning.

Can VRF be phased in an occupied building?

Yes. Systems are typically split by floor or wing, so we can replace and commission one zone at a time while the rest of the building stays occupied.

What maintenance does a VRF system need?

Filter and coil cleaning on indoor units, condenser coil cleaning, periodic refrigerant leak checks and controller firmware reviews. Our service plans cover all of these.
